Privacy Policy — NotitApp

Last updated: September 2026

NotitApp is a note-taking app. It works entirely offline: there is no account, no login, and no network connection of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. NotitApp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or analytics. There are no servers involved — every note and category you create is stored locally on your device (via Apple's SwiftData framework) and never leaves it.

Third-party services

NotitApp does not use any third-party service, SDK, or library that processes data on our behalf.

Data deletion

All data lives only on your device. Deleting a note or category in the app removes it immediately and permanently. Deleting the app removes everything else.
