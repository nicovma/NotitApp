Privacy Policy — NotitApp

Last updated: October 2026

NotitApp is a note-taking app. There is no account and no login — your notes and categories are stored only on your device.

Your notes and categories

All notes and categories you create are stored locally on your device via Apple's SwiftData framework. They are never uploaded to any server and never leave your device through normal use of the app.

On-device AI suggestions

NotitApp can suggest a title and category for a note using Apple Intelligence's on-device language model (Apple's FoundationModels framework). This processing happens entirely on your device — the text of your note is never sent to Apple, to us, or to any other server for this feature.

Crash reports and usage analytics

NotitApp uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google) to help us find and fix bugs and understand how the app is used. This can include:

  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics (device type, OS version, stack traces).
  • Basic usage events, such as when a note or category is created — this includes the name of the category involved (e.g. "Trabajo"), but never the content of your notes.

None of this data is linked to your identity — NotitApp has no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to tie this data back to a specific person. It is used only in aggregate, to improve the app.

Data deletion

Notes and categories: deleting them in the app removes them immediately and permanently from your device. Deleting the app removes everything else stored locally. Crash/analytics data already sent to Firebase is retained and deleted according to Google's standard Firebase data retention policies, and is not linked to an identifiable person.
