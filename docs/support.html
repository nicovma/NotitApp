Frequently asked

Where are my notes stored?
Only on your device. NotitApp has no account and no cloud sync, so notes don't transfer automatically if you switch devices.

Why don't I see AI suggestions when creating a note?
That feature uses Apple Intelligence, which requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings. If it's unavailable, the app tells you why directly in the note editor.

How do I delete a note or category?
Swipe left on it in the list, or open it and use the delete button. Deleting a category also deletes its notes.
